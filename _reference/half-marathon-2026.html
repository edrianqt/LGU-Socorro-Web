1st Bucas Grande International Half Marathon
2026-02-22
By : LGU Socorro

1ST BUCAS GRANDE INTERNATIONAL HALF MARATHON

Location: Socorro, Bucas Grande Island in Siargao, Surigao del Norte
Date: February 22, 2026


Run, and be enchanted, at the 1st Bucas Grande International Half Marathon in Socorro, Surigao del Norte. With race categories 21 KM, 10 KM, and 5 KM, there is a distance for both seasoned racers and first time island runners.

More than a race, this is a full Bucas Grande experience. Hydration and aid stations will feature seafood galore, local delicacies, and energy boosting beverages. Along the route you will encounter Socorro-style hospitality and charm. The 2026 21 KM medal is also the first piece of a three-year trilogy, created so runners will return and let Bucas Grande be part of their story each year.

Come run, explore, and immerse yourself in Bucas Grande. Add the optional Bucas Grande island tour during registration if you want the full eco adventure with local guides. Whether you are chasing a personal best or simply want to enjoy a beautiful course by the sea, the Bucas Grande International Half Marathon will give you a race and an island experience you will never forget.
