Mabuhay!

Welcome to our beautiful island municipality of Socorro. It is my honor to invite you to explore our website and discover the wonders of Bucas Grande, our island home.

We, the Team Mabungahong Socorro, envision our island to be the premier eco-tourism and agri-fishery hub of the Philippines, where natural beauty, sustainable practices, and community-driven initiatives come together to create a thriving and resilient community.

Our island’s stunning landscapes and seascapes, rich biodiversity, vibrant culture, timeless tradition, and warm hospitality make it an ideal destination for eco-tourists and nature lovers. Our agri-fishery sector, meanwhile, provides a bounty of fresh produce and seafood, showcasing the best of our island’s resources.

On this website, you’ll find information about our tourism attractions, agricultural and fishery initiatives, community programs, and more. We’re committed to transparency, accountability, and citizen engagement, and we invite you to join the conversation.

Let’s work together to build a brighter future for our island community. Maraming salamat (thank you) for visiting, and I look forward to welcoming you to our island soon!

RIZA RAFONSELLE T. TIMCANG
Municipal Mayor

Welcome to Socorro!

It is with great pride and heartfelt dedication that I welcome you to the official website of the Island Municipality of Socorro—a land blessed with natural beauty, rich cultural heritage, and a resilient, hardworking people.

As Vice-Mayor and Presiding Officer of the Sangguniang Bayan, I fully and wholeheartedly support our collective vision of transforming Socorro into the premier eco-tourism and agri-fishery hub of the Philippines. This is more than just a dream—it is a shared commitment that demands unity, innovation, and strong legislative foundations.

In partnership with our Municipal Mayor, the Sangguniang Bayan remains steadfast in crafting responsive and progressive policies that promote sustainable tourism, protect our environment, and empower our farmers and fisherfolk. We are working to ensure that our laws reflect the needs of the times while staying true to the values that make Socorro a beacon of hope and opportunity in our region.

Let this website serve not only as a portal of information, but as a symbol of transparency, cooperation, and shared progress. I invite all Socorronhons—here and abroad—as well as visitors and partners, to join us in this journey toward inclusive growth and ecological sustainability.

Together, let us build a brighter and greener future for Socorro.

Mabuhay ang Isla sa Paglaum!

DALISAY R. GALANIDA-CANTA
Municipal Vice Mayor
