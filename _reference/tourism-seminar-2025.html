Successful 3-Day Tour Package Development & Delivery Seminar

Posted on July 24, 2025December 7, 2025    

Municipality of Socorro: SUCCESSFUL 3-DAY TOUR PACKAGE DEVELOPMENT & DELIVERY SEMINAR!
July 14–16, 2025 | Women’s Center, Socorro, Surigao del Norte🫶🫶🫶

A huge congratulations to all the Tour Operators, Accommodation Establishments, and Tourism Stakeholders who participated in the impactful 3-Day Tour Package Development and Delivery Seminar! This immersive training brought out the best in every attendee—culminating in exceptional outputs that showcased their creativity, collaboration, and passion for tourism development.

Over the course of the seminar, participants engaged in in-depth learning sessions, hands-on write-shops, mock tour simulation, presentations of tour packages, and constructive critiquing and feedback. Indeed, the energy was vibrant, the collaboration was inspiring, and the results were truly mabungahon!❤️

Our deepest gratitude to the brilliant Dr. Freddie Quinito, whose expertise and guidance elevated the entire learning experience. A warm thank-you as well to DOT Caraga Region XIII, ably led by RD Von Caraga Beyond Its Waves for their strong support, and to our ever-supportive local officials, Mayor Riza Rafonselle Taruc – Timcang, Vice Mayor Dalisay Canta, and the Mabungahong Socorro leadership for making this meaningful event possible.🙏

Together, we are building stronger, more sustainable, and experience-rich tourism for Caraga and beyond!❤️🥳

Source: Municipality of Socorro
